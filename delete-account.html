Nyx Sleep Pause

Delete your account & data

This page explains how to delete your Nyx Sleep Pause account and any associated data.

Nyx is built to keep your data on your device. The only information that ever leaves your phone is a sign-in identifier (via Google Sign-In), and only if you choose to sign in to control your Google Home devices. Everything else — your pause log, settings, and heart-rate readings — stays on your phone and watch, and is never uploaded.

To delete your account (the Google sign-in)

If you signed in with Google in Nyx, you can remove that connection at any time:

  1. Open Nyx Sleep Pause on your phone, go to Settings, and tap Sign out. This disconnects your Google account from the app.
  2. To fully revoke Nyx’s access, visit your Google Account → Third-party apps & services, select Nyx Sleep Pause, and choose Remove access.

Once removed, Nyx no longer holds any sign-in identifier for you.

To delete all app data

  1. Uninstall Nyx Sleep Pause from your phone (and the watch). This permanently removes the local database — your pause log, settings, and all on-device data.
  2. Alternatively, in Android Settings → Apps → Nyx Sleep Pause → Storage, tap Clear data to wipe the same on-device data without uninstalling.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: all on-device data (pause log, settings) when you uninstall or clear data; the Google sign-in connection when you sign out / remove access.
  • Kept: nothing. Nyx does not retain personal data on its own servers — there is no cloud copy of your pause history or heart rate, because that data never leaves your device. Heart rate is used only momentarily on the watch to detect sleep and is never stored.
  • Retention: none beyond your device. Removing the sign-in (above) takes effect immediately.
