Nyx Sleep Pause

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Nyx Sleep Pause (“Nyx”, “the app”, “we”) is an Android and Wear OS app that automatically pauses your media when it detects you have fallen asleep. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices.

We have tried to keep this honest and short. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the email at the bottom.

The short version

  • Nyx reads your heart rate on your watch to detect sleep. This stays on your devices — we do not collect or sell it.
  • Nyx needs notification access on your phone so it can see and pause the media that is currently playing. It does not read the content of your notifications for any other purpose.
  • If you choose to connect Google Home, Nyx can pause media on your Home devices (TVs, speakers). This uses Google’s APIs under your own account.
  • A small event log (e.g. “paused Netflix at 00:18”) is stored on your device, and optionally synced to your private cloud space so a partner you invite can see it. You control this.
  • We use Firebase (a Google service) for optional sign-in and that sync.

What data Nyx handles

Health / body sensors (heart rate, on the watch). The Wear OS app reads heart-rate samples via Android Health Services to decide when you have likely fallen asleep. These samples are processed on the watch and are not transmitted off your devices and not stored by us. Only the resulting “sleep event” signal (a timestamp) is sent to your phone over the local Wearable Data Layer.

Media session information (on the phone). To pause what is playing, Nyx uses Android’s notification-listener access to read active media sessions. It records the title and playback position of media it pauses (e.g. “The Left Hand of Darkness — paused at 02:11”) so it can show you a log. It does not read, store, or transmit the contents of your other notifications.

Account information (optional). If you sign in with Google (to connect Google Home or to sync across your household), we receive a Google account identifier through Firebase Authentication. If you do not sign in, the app uses an anonymous identifier.

Pause event log. A list of pause events (timestamp, app/source, media title, heart-rate at the moment of pause) is stored locally on your device in a database. If you enable household sync, this log is stored in your private Firebase Firestore space and shared only with members you explicitly invite by their user ID.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not transmit raw heart-rate data off your devices.
  • We do not share your data with third parties other than the Google services described here that operate on your behalf.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Cloud Firestore) — optional sign-in and household sync. Governed by Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Home APIs — only if you connect Google Home; used to pause media on your own Home devices under your account.

Permissions and why

  • Body sensors / heart rate (watch): detect sleep.
  • Notification access (phone): see and pause active media.
  • Foreground service (media playback / health): keep pause + detection running reliably.
  • Set alarm: optional “set a sleep timer” smart action, only if you enable it.

Your choices

  • Use Nyx without signing in (anonymous, local only).
  • Disable household sync to keep all event data on-device.
  • Revoke notification access or sensor permission at any time in Android Settings (the relevant feature stops working, but the app still runs).
  • Uninstalling removes the local database from your device.

Data retention & deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. For synced household data, contact us at the email below to request deletion of your cloud-stored events.

Children

Nyx is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
